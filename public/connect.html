Put your store in every shopper's pocket

SuperX connects your Shopify store to Shoppy — a shopping copilot on WhatsApp. When shoppers ask for products you carry, Shoppy answers with your live prices, stock, and a link straight to checkout.

2-minute setup, zero maintenance. One click of approval — your catalog syncs itself from then on, every price and stock change, in real time.

High-intent reach. Shoppers on SuperX are asking to buy — your products get quoted at the exact moment of decision, with a direct link.
